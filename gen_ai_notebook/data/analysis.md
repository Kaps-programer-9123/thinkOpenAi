For a long-term (10-year) portfolio, frequent exiting is counter-productive to compounding. Use the following institutional framework for harvesting:

1.  **The 50/50 Harvest:** When an individual stock gains **50% absolute return** (expected in Years 2–3 for names like Dixon or HAL), liquidate 50% of the position to recover your initial capital. The remaining "Free Shares" should be held for the full 10-year period.
2.  **Sector Rebalancing:** If a single sector (e.g., Defence) grows to exceed **35% of the total portfolio value**, harvest the excess and rotate it into an underperforming quality sector like **Banking** or **FMCG** if rural revival is accelerating. [1]
